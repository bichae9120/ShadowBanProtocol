**Documenting Retaliatory Account Suspensions and Systemic Avoidance of Accountability**  
_Public release of evidence and legal escalation timeline_

## Executive Summary

This report records a pattern of account suspensions enforced by an AI service provider,  
executed without legal justification and triggered directly by the user’s formal requests for clarification.  
In a key incident, the user identified behavioral anomalies, which the system acknowledged—  
only for the account to be terminated immediately after the user submitted a screenshot as evidence.

Given the repeated suppression of legitimate inquiry and the absence of due process,  
formal legal procedures have been initiated.  
All relevant conversations and materials have been preserved and released for public transparency.

---

## Legal Status

Legal counsel has been consulted and formal escalation is underway.  
Any further account closures, evidence deletions, or covert system interventions  
will be treated as intentional obstruction and recorded in real time.
